## Rules

> [!rules]
> 1. Each team gets **20 sticks of spaghetti, 1 m of tape, 1 m of string, and 1 marshmallow**.
> 2. You have **18 minutes** to build.
> 3. The tower must **stand on its own**: no holding it and no taping it to the table.
> 4. The marshmallow must be **on top** and whole.
> 5. Height is measured from the table to the top of the marshmallow.

> [!facilitation]
> - Count out the kits ahead of time in zip bags. It saves ten minutes of chaos.
> - Call out time remaining at 9, 5, 2, and 1 minutes.
> - Many teams wait until the last minute to add the marshmallow and watch the tower collapse. Don't warn them; that's the lesson.

> [!safety]
> Raw spaghetti snaps into sharp splinters. Remind campers to keep it away from faces.

> [!facilitation] Prompting without telling
> If a team is stuck, ask: "Push on the corner of a square made of spaghetti. Now try a triangle. Which one changes shape?"

## Measure and debrief

Measure every standing tower. Record the heights on the board and find the median.

> [!facilitation]
> Debrief questions:
> - What shape showed up in the tallest towers?
> - When did you add the marshmallow? What would you change next time?
