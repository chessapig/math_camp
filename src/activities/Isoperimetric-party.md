Campers will work together to solve isoperimetric problems, through a series of games.
## Games

### Bag of marbles
> [!rules] How to play
> Setup a large circle made out of rope, tied with a slipnot on one end. The campers must stand inside the circle, spaced apart so that they cannot touch another camper's body with their arms. Then, tighten the slipknot, pulling the campers together. How tight can the campers become? 

> [!rules] Tree variation
> Same game, but now the rope also surrounds two trees (about 20 feet apart)

The next variation is based on **Dido's problem**
>After the assassination of her husband by her brother, queen Dido fled to a haven near Tunis. There she asked the local leader, Yarb, for as much land as could be enclosed by the hide of a bull. Since the deal seemed very modest, he agreed. Dido cut the hide into narrow strips, tied them together excised a large tract of land along the sea which became the city of Carthage.

Queen dido sought to contain the most area in a fixed perimeter, using the sea as a barrier. Back In the 21st century, the wall will represent the sea, and their rope is the oxhide Give students some string and let them try to find their own answer. After this, play the wall variation. 

> [!rules] Wall variation
> play the game against a wall. tie one end of the rope to one point on the wall, and have a facilitator hold the other end of the rope against the wall. 

### Fences

> [!figure]  
> ![A group of people, each standing on their own blob in the ground. Sone kneels beside, placing their own blob](isoperimetric/fences.jpg)


Give each camper a 6 foot length of string. Each camper lays the string on the ground to make a "fence" enclosing themselves. Challenge the campers to arrange the string to give the most area inside of their enclosure. Then, let campers use other people's string with their own. At the end, there is a large conglomeration of string blobs, with every camper fenced in in their own enclosure. Challenge the campers to make the total enclosure as large as possible.

>[!tip] Variations 
> - **Wall variation**: Same game, but against a wall. The wall may be used as part of each camper's enclosure
> - **Greedy variation:** Challenge the campers to make their own enclosure as large as possible. They are only allowed to move their own rope. 
> - **Asymmetric variation:** Can you make one person have a very large enclosure, where everyone else has small enclosures?

Discuss how the optimal solution is the honeycomb pattern

### Slime mold

> [!figure]  
> ![A grove of trees, with a chain of people arm to arm stretching from tree to tree.](isoperimetric/slime_mold.jpg)
Place a bunch of orange cones around a field. (If you have access to it, you can use a grove of trees instead). Challenge campers to hold hands and form chains, to touch as many cones as possible. Campers are encouraged to form branches, the only rule is that every camper must be connected to the same blob.
